Faith Clinic Foundation

Who we are and why we serve

Our story

A foundation built around dignity, opportunity and action.

Faith Clinic Foundation is a nonprofit nongovernmental organization focused on helping underserved people and communities in Nigeria.

Our work centres on children and young people while responding to practical needs in families and communities. We believe education, health and economic opportunity can change the direction of a life.

01

Our vision

A society where everyone has meaningful access to basic necessities and the opportunity to build a better future.

Our approach

Listen to communities, identify practical needs, mobilise partners and deliver support with accountability.

🎯

Mission

To provide practical support and opportunities that help vulnerable children, families and young people move toward a safer and more hopeful future.

❤

Values

Dignity, compassion, integrity, inclusion, accountability and community partnership guide our work.

🌍

Our reach

We work with communities and partners in Nigeria, with a long-term ambition to contribute to positive change across Africa.
