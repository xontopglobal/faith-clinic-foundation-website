Who we are

A foundation built around dignity, opportunity and action.

Faith Clinic Foundation is a nonprofit nongovernmental organization focused on helping people who are underserved and at risk of being left behind.

Our work centres on children and young people, while also responding to practical needs in families and communities. We believe that education, health and economic opportunity can change the direction of a life.

Our vision

A society where everyone has meaningful access to basic necessities and the opportunity to build a better future.

Our approach

Community impact

Turning concern into action.

Our community work has included education outreach, school support, children’s activities, healthcare and assistance for vulnerable people.

Education outreach

Giving children tools to keep learning.

Our outreach activities have provided school materials and encouragement to children in underserved communities, helping make the return to school more achievable.

Amandugba & community education

Supporting children where the need is greatest.

Faith Clinic Foundation has worked with communities including Amandugba in Imo State to promote educational opportunity and respond to barriers faced by children and families.
